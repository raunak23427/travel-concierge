## What Wayzyy is

Wayzyy Technologies Private Limited. GSTIN 09AAECW5169M1ZL. Contact: hello@wayzyy.com; hosts/partnerships: akshaykumar.sharma@wayzyy.com.

Tagline: _"cozy stays, crazy nights and fair hosting. That's wayzyy."_ Also: _"stays without the small print."_

An India-native homestay and villa rental marketplace, **Goa first**, currently **pre-launch (waitlist phase)**.

**The core inversion:** every OTA is a toll booth that skims each booking. Wayzyy is a prepaid top-up meter — hosts buy credit packs up front (like recharging a SIM), which unlock a set amount of booking _value_. Zero per-booking percentage, ever.

### The Airbnb 15.5% argument (their strongest content asset)

Airbnb moved from split fees (3% host + 14–16.5% guest) to a flat 15.5% host-only fee.

- Old model: to net Rs 10,000/night, host lists at Rs 10,309; guest pays Rs 11,773 at checkout
- New model: to net the same Rs 10,000, host must list at **Rs 11,834** — a 15.5% jump in the search-visible price
- India-specific damage: price-sensitive guests filter by budget bracket. A villa moving Rs 10,000 → Rs 11,500 leaves its competitive bracket and lands against premium listings → occupancy drops
- Psychological: the fee is now hidden _inside_ the base rate, so guests blame the host for overcharging
- Their money quote: _"Guests stopped seeing the fee, but we didn't stop paying it."_

## The trust thesis — why they say they're building it

Their homepage leads with host abandonment, not price. Canonical story: a Superhost of ~10 years, 5,000+ reviews, 4.88 average, lost to a refund-for-review extortion play.

Four named failure modes:

1. **Refund-for-review extortion** — _"Refund me or I'll feel obligated to mention this in my review."_
2. **Staged evidence beats time-stamped proof** — 2am photo set + replaced bedding + sympathetic agent = penalty-free cancellation even against clean before-photos
3. **The host's warning gets deleted** — honest warnings pulled as "retaliatory"; the lying guest's 1-star stays up
4. **"The decision is final."** — Trust & Safety unreachable, receipts irrelevant

Closing line, attributed to "EVERY HOST EVENTUALLY": _"I didn't lose money I can't recover. What I lost is trust in the platform. After ten years of hosting — I'm out."_

Stated countermeasures: DigiLocker/Aadhaar verification before booking; **manual review of any rating ≤3 stars before publication**; three-layer mitigation (prevention → mediation → evidence-based resolution); no algorithmic visibility suppression after bad ratings; platform-wide blacklisting of repeat offenders; staff review of new listings.

| Airbnb FY2024       | Value                                 |

**Airbnb spends 2.57% of booking value on marketing alone. Wayzyy's entire gross revenue is 2.0% of booking value.**

Wayzyy cannot buy demand — not "shouldn't", _cannot_. Matching Airbnb's marketing intensity proportionally (19% of revenue) gives Wayzyy 0.38% of GMV, roughly **one seventh** of Airbnb's per-booking firepower. Every rupee of Wayzyy growth must come from a channel that is free, organic, or funded by someone else. This constraint should govern the entire roadmap.

## `[EXTERNAL]` Off-platform leakage and channel stickiness

From an analysis of 231,150 confirmed bookings across 115 operators (2025):

- Repeat guests = 5–8% of revenue typically, up to 26% for some operators
- **77% of repeat-guest revenue still flows through OTAs** — operators pay >$1bn/yr in OTA fees on guests they already earned
- Operators with <5% direct share lose ~90% of repeat revenue to OTAs; operators at 30%+ direct share cut leakage to ~10%
- **Channel stickiness: 86% of guests who first booked direct return direct; ~89% who first booked Airbnb return via Airbnb**
- Well-run direct strategies reach 40–60% direct within 24 months

**Implication: the first booking decides the lifetime relationship.**

## `[EXTERNAL]` Goa market

- **9,684 active STR listings** in Goa; median annual revenue **Rs 6.53 lakh**; occupancy 46% (Feb 2025–Jan 2026, Airbtics)
- Implied Goa STR GMV ≈ **Rs 632 crore/yr** → Rs 12.6 Cr at a 2% take rate. Goa alone cannot support a venture-scale business at 2%.
- Wayzyy's own 500 properties × Rs 6.53L = **Rs 32.65 Cr** addressable GMV → **Rs 65.3 lakh/yr** at 2%. That is the entire company today.
- North Goa luxury micro-markets (Assagao, Siolim, Morjim): 25–30% annual price appreciation, 8–9% rental yields
- **2025 Goa arrivals: 1,08,02,410** — 95.2% domestic. Foreign arrivals falling; domestic driving all growth.
- Less seasonal than assumed: Jan 10.56L peak, but Apr 8.42L / May 9.27L / Jun 8.34L
- India vacation rental market: $3.45bn (2025) → $19.94bn (2035), 19.18% CAGR
- 87.5% of India's travel search/booking happens in apps — **and Wayzyy has no app yet**

### How Goa villas actually get booked `[critical]`

Not through OTA search. Through **Instagram DM → WhatsApp**. A one-page site + WhatsApp button + Google Business Profile generates direct enquiries within weeks; **a villa that answers every DM within the hour converts 3× more enquiries**. The 35–55 Mumbai/Pune family segment still discovers heavily via Facebook.

**Implication: Wayzyy's competitor for the guest relationship is Instagram, not Airbnb. Hosts already own demand.**

## Regulation — Goa Homestay & B&B Scheme 2025

Notified 23 Oct 2025, valid 5 years. **Registration with the Department of Tourism is mandatory for all operators**, statewide. Active crackdown on unregistered stays; the government is monitoring online travel platforms for compliance. Precedent: Yuvraj Singh served a tourism-department notice for renting his Goa villa.

### Tax

- GST 12% on rooms below Rs 7,500/night, 18% above
- Rs 20 lakh annual turnover threshold for GST registration
- §9(5) CGST: when accommodation is supplied through an e-commerce operator, **the platform collects and pays the GST**, relieving the host. Booking off-platform pushes that liability back onto the host.
- TCS 1% collected by platforms

## Structural vulnerability — the thing their own site does not say

Their ~2% claim holds only **if the booking is routed through Wayzyy**. The revenue model has no transaction chokepoint:

- Airbnb collects at transaction time. A leaked booking costs Airbnb one commission.
- Wayzyy collects up front against a quota. A leaked booking suppresses recharge frequency — which _is_ the revenue.
- Once a host's credit pack is consumed, the marginal cost of the next on-platform booking is a fresh recharge — creating a **peak incentive to go off-platform exactly when host volume is highest. The best hosts have the strongest reason to leak.**
- UPI is host-direct-friendly by design; a host can be paid off-platform in seconds with no card rails to intercept.

**The inversion:** leakage is normally an unfixable tax. For Wayzyy it is a weapon Airbnb structurally cannot copy. Airbnb charges 15.5% on repeat guests and can never stop, because commission _is_ the business. Wayzyy's revenue is already decoupled from transactions, so it can price repeat and host-originated direct bookings at ~0% and absorb the direct-booking channel instead of fighting it.
