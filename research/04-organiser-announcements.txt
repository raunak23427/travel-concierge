The PPT Submission round for *Geeks2Code* opens tomorrow. 
Please read the guidelines below carefully to ensure a smooth submission process on Unstop.

*Timeline & Portal Details*
* *Submission Window:* September 12, 2026 – September 14, 2026
* *Platform:* Submit directly on the Unstop portal via your team dashboard.
* *File Format:* PDF or PPTX format is preferred.
---
*Themes & Problem Statements*
* *Open Innovation:* Geeks2Code follows an Open Innovation approach. 
You are free to choose your own problem statement or address any real-world problem across any tech domain.
* *Wayzyy Special Track (Hiring & TalentTech):* Alternatively, you can choose to solve practical challenges in recruitment, talent discovery, and hiring provided by Wayzyy.
* *Where to find it:* Go to the *Themes/Domains* section on Unstop and scroll down to *Special Problem Statement*.
* *Perks:* Standout teams in this track may be considered for potential hiring opportunities with Wayzyy.
---
*Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)*
1. *Do we have to pick an Unstop problem statement, or can we choose our own?*
You are completely free to choose your own problem statement and build an original solution. Working on the provided Unstop/Wayzyy problem statements is entirely optional.

2. *Is there a mandatory PPT template or slide limit?*
No fixed template or slide limit exists. You are free to choose your own layout, theme, and design. Adding GFG logos or mentions is optional.

3. *What key sections must be included in the PPT?*
Your presentation should clearly cover:

* Problem Statement
* Proposed Solution
* Innovation & Uniqueness (USP)
* Technology Stack & Technical Approach
* Implementation Plan
* Expected Impact

4. *Can we submit a conceptual project?*
No. Submissions must demonstrate real technical implementation and a practical execution plan—purely conceptual presentations will not pass.

5. *What are the evaluation criteria?*
Projects are judged on innovation, problem relevance, technical feasibility, potential impact, and presentation clarity. Shortlisted teams move to Round 2 (Online Mentoring).

6. *What is the plagiarism policy?*
Plagiarism, copying existing projects, or submitting previously built work as new will result in immediate disqualification.
